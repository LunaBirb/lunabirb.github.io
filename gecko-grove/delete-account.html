Delete your account

This page is about the Android game Gecko Grove: Gecko Breeding on Google Play (app ID geckogrove.game), made by Glittersquid. If you signed in with Google in the game, you have an account, and you can delete it and everything kept with it at any time.

The quick way: in the game

  1. Open Gecko Grove and tap Settings (the gear).
  2. Scroll to Online and tap Delete account, at the bottom.
  3. Tap the red Delete account button. If Google asks, choose your Google account once more.

It's done in a few seconds, while you watch. If your connection drops half-way, the game finishes the job the next time you open it.

Don't have the game any more? Ask by email

Write to hybrid4@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, so we know the account is yours. You can copy this:

Subject: Delete my Gecko Grove account

Please delete my Gecko Grove account and all the data kept with it.
I am writing from the email address of the Google account I signed in with.

Write the email

We delete it by hand and reply when it's done: usually within a few days, and always within 30 days. We don't need to know why, and we won't ask you for anything else.

What is deleted, and what isn't

Deleted

  • Your breeder card: your breeder name, your picture and showcase geckos, your studs, your numbers and badges.
  • Your leaderboard scores, on every board, and your Sunday Show entry.
  • Your friends list, your list of blocked players, any parcels waiting for you, your guest book, and the picture of your tank if you shared one.
  • Any trade that is still open. It is closed first, so no gecko has moved and your friend isn't left waiting.
  • Your cloud save: the copy of your grove kept in your account, and any earlier copy kept beside it.
  • Your sign-in: the record of your Google account's email address, name and ID that let you sign in.

Not deleted

  • The grove on your phone. It lives on your device, not with us. To remove it too, use Settings › Reset game, or uninstall the game.
  • Reports you made about other players' cards. They hold no name or email, only an ID that no longer belongs to anyone, and they are kept so that hidden cards stay hidden.
  • Small things you already sent to other players that their game hasn't put away yet: a parcel's sticker, a heart, a line in a guest book. They hold no name, and each is cleared by the other player's game. Your friends simply stop seeing you in their list.
  • Geckos you traded away. They live in your friend's grove, on your friend's phone: they are theirs now. A finished trade that your friend's game hasn't collected yet stays until it has (it holds the gecko they are owed, and its name), and is then cleared.
  • Your Google account itself, and anything you bought through Google Play. Those belong to your Google account.

Deleted data leaves our database at once. Google, which runs the database for us, removes it from its backup systems within 180 days.

Only want to leave the leaderboards?

You don't have to delete your account for that. In the game, open Settings › Online › Breeder card and switch Show my card off. Your card and every score are deleted straight away, and your cloud save stays.

If you just stop playing

You don't have to do anything. If an account isn't used for two years, we delete the account and everything kept with it, exactly as if you had asked: your breeder card and scores, your friends list, anything waiting for you from other players, the picture of your tank, your cloud save and your sign-in. The grove on your phone isn't touched, and if you come back you can sign in and back it up again.

Children

Gecko Grove doesn't offer accounts to children, and collects nothing from them. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has an account anyway, write to the address above and we'll delete it.
